Design a relational database for a ride-sharing platform similar to Uber.

The system should support the following features:

1. Passengers who can register, login, and request rides.
2. Drivers who register with their vehicle details.
3. Vehicles associated with drivers including vehicle type and registration number.
4. Ride requests made by passengers including pickup and drop locations.
5. Ride status tracking such as requested, accepted, in-progress, and completed.
6. Payments for completed rides with payment method and transaction details.
7. Ratings and reviews given by passengers to drivers after a ride.

The database should include proper primary keys, foreign keys, and normalized tables.
